# Watch a parent use Brandon Valley Lunch

Ten minutes per parent, on their own phone, with their own students set
up. Hand them this list one line at a time. Say nothing else. Write down
where they pause for more than a few seconds, tap the wrong thing, or
ask a question. Those three things are the findings.

Pick parents from different levels: one elementary, one middle, one high
school. A parent with two children at different schools is worth two.

## The tasks

1. Is there school tomorrow? What time does it start?
2. What's for lunch tomorrow? Now find the whole menu, with the sides.
3. When is the next game? Is it home or away?
4. Your child will be out sick tomorrow. Tell the school.
5. Call the school office.
6. It's a two-hour late start. What time does first period begin?
7. Which days are off this month? When does winter break start?
8. Find the supply list (elementary) or the handbook rule about medicine at school.
9. Put the games on your phone's calendar.
10. Turn on the evening heads-up about tomorrow.
11. Add a second child. Then switch between them.
12. Set up the other parent's phone with the same students.
13. Switch the app to Spanish and back.
14. Ask the app a question in your own words.

## What to write down

- Task number, and what they tapped first.
- Any pause longer than a few seconds, and what was on the screen.
- Any wrong tap, and where it took them.
- Anything they said out loud, especially "where is..." or "what does ... mean".
- Whether they finished without help.

## Afterward, ask only these

- What did you expect to find that wasn't there?
- Which word in the app didn't mean anything to you?
- What would you use this for most?
